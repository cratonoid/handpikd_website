When to Gift & What to Give

Event-by-event quick reference guide

🎓

Orientation & Welcome

Recipients: Incoming students

Best Gifts: Pens, notebooks, water bottles, keychains

Budget: ₹100–₹300

🏆

Convocation & Graduation

Recipients: Graduating students

Best Gifts: Premium notebooks, tumblers, wireless chargers

Budget: ₹700–₹1,500

🤝

Placements & Recruiters

Recipients: Recruiters, placed students

Best Gifts: Bottles, pen sets, desk accessories

Budget: ₹300–₹3,500

👩‍🏫

Teacher's Day & Faculty

Recipients: Faculty and staff

Best Gifts: Leather items, copper bottles, wellness sets

Budget: ₹700–₹1,500

🌐

MoU Signings & Partnerships

Recipients: Corporate partners, officials

Best Gifts: Luxury sets, leather goods, executive hampers

Budget: ₹1,500–₹5,000+

🎉

Fests & Events

Recipients: Participants, volunteers, winners

Best Gifts: T-shirts, caps, bottles, tote bags

Budget: ₹100–₹400

📚

Guest Lectures & Seminars

Recipients: Expert speakers, visiting faculty

Best Gifts: Premium notebooks, pen sets, portfolios

Budget: ₹1,000–₹2,000

🏅

Sports & Inter-college

Recipients: Winners, participants, teams

Best Gifts: Water bottles, earphones, apparel

Budget: ₹200–₹1,500

🎁

Festive Gifting

Recipients: Students, faculty, alumni, recruiters

Best Gifts: Hampers, wellness kits, eco sets

Budget: ₹300–₹1,500

💼

Chief Guests & VIPs

Recipients: Dignitaries, officials, board members

Best Gifts: Curated luxury sets, bespoke items

Budget: ₹2,500–₹5,000+

🎓

Alumni & Homecoming

Recipients: Alumni, donors, class reps

Best Gifts: Commemorative items, premium sets

Budget: ₹500–₹2,000

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Parent Events & Admissions

Recipients: Parents, prospective families

Best Gifts: Notebooks, pens, branded totes

Budget: ₹250–₹600
